## Common report workflow

**Where to go:** Reports → Construction Reports

1. Choose a report card.
2. Select Corporation and Project where required.
3. Select vendor, status, or date range if available.
4. Select **Show**.
5. Review totals and detail.
6. Use **Export Excel** or **Print** when provided.

If a report is empty, confirm required filters and date range before escalating.

## 10.1 Detailed Budget Report

**Business question:** How does project budget compare with PO/CO commitments, paid cost, and remaining budget?

Key columns include Budgeted Amount, PO Amount, Change Order Amount, Total Amount, Paid Amount, and Budget Remaining by cost code/division.

## 10.2 PO Details with Invoice Summary

**Business question:** For each PO/CO, how much is committed, invoiced, held back, paid, and still payable?

Key columns include Goods Amount, Freight, Additional Charges, Tax, PO Total, Total Invoiced, Holdback, Total Paid, Balance to be Paid, and PO Status.

## 10.3 Material Purchase Order Breakout

**Business question:** Which material items were ordered, invoiced, and received by category, item type, specification, location, and UOM?

Use it for detailed material commitment and fulfillment review.

## 10.4 Labor Purchase Order Breakout

**Business question:** Which labor cost codes and vendors hold project commitments, including labor change orders and invoicing?

## 10.5 Vendor Accounts Payable Summary

**Business question:** What is the contract, invoiced, holdback, tax, paid, and AP balance by vendor?

This is the primary vendor-level payable summary for a project and period.

## 10.6 Stock Report

**Business question:** What material is in shipment, received/current stock, returned, or otherwise available for the project?

Receipt Note Shipment/Received status directly affects stock buckets.

## 10.7 PO-wise Stock Report

**Business question:** How is stock distributed by Purchase Order and vendor?

Use vendor and project filters to trace stock back to a source PO.

## 10.8 AP Aging

AP Aging groups outstanding payables into Current/0–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ day buckets by vendor.

This report currently has a direct page but may not appear as a card on the Reports hub. It offers Refresh and Export; availability depends on deployment/navigation.

## Reporting tips

- Export files reflect the filters applied at generation time.
- Print layouts hide interactive controls.
- Currency-converted documents may show both document and corporation currency.
- “No data” can mean no qualifying Approved/Paid transaction in the selected period, not necessarily an application error.
