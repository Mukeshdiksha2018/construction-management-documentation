## Purpose

A Stock Receipt Note records material shipped or received against an approved Material PO or CO.

## 8.1 Receipt Note list

**Where to go:** Const Orders → Receipt Notes

The list groups notes under their PO/CO and can filter by project, vendor, date, and Shipment/Received status.

![Screenshot UG-GRN-01 — Const Orders Stock Receipt list](/images/UG-GRN-01.png)

## 8.2 Create a Receipt Note

1. Select **Add Receipt Note**.
2. Choose Purchase Order or Change Order.
3. Select an Approved or Partially Received material source.
4. Confirm vendor, project, dates, GRN number, received-by, and location.
5. Choose source lines.
6. Enter received quantities.
7. Review item total, charges, taxes, and total received amount.
8. Select Shipment or Received.
9. Save & Close or Save & New.

Save & Close closes the form immediately; the final success or failure appears as a toast.

![Screenshot UG-GRN-02 — Create / Edit Stock Receipt Note form](/images/UG-GRN-02.png)

## 8.3 Shipment versus Received

- **Shipment:** quantity is shown as in transit/in shipment in stock reporting.
- **Received:** quantity is shown as current stock.

PO/CO completion is calculated from saved receipt-line quantities, not only from the GRN label.

## 8.4 Quantity validation

- At least one line must have received quantity above zero.
- A quantity above the calculated leftover is highlighted and blocked by the form.
- Fully fulfilled lines are hidden from new item selection.
- If only a subset of source lines is included, the source remains Partially Received.

> Over-receipt is currently prevented by the user interface. Escalate suspected concurrent or non-UI over-receipt cases for data review.

## 8.5 Source status

After save:

- No receipt quantity → Approved.
- Some quantity → Partially Received.
- All receivable lines fulfilled → Completed.

Deleting a GRN can move the source back to Partially Received or Approved.
