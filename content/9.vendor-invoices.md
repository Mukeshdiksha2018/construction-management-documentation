## Purpose

Vendor Invoices record direct costs, invoices against POs/COs, advance payments, and holdback releases.

## 9.1 Vendor Invoice list

**Where to go:** Const Orders → Vendor Invoices

Search by invoice, vendor, project, type, or order number. Filter by project, vendor, status, invoice type, and bill-date range.

Columns include Bill Date, Invoice Number, Project, Vendor, Invoice Type, Due Date, Order Number, Amount, Status, and Actions.

![Screenshot UG-VI-01 — Const Orders Vendor Invoices list](/images/UG-VI-01.png)

## 9.2 Invoice types

### Enter Direct Invoice

Use when no PO or CO is required. Enter or import project lines, allocate cost codes, and complete the financial breakdown.

### Against PO

Select a PO, load remaining material/labor lines, enter invoiced quantities/amounts, apply advance or holdback, and complete the financial breakdown.

### Against CO

Works like Against PO but uses Change Order lines and balances.

### Against Advance Payment

Records an advance and distributes it by cost code. Later PO/CO invoices can apply adjusted advance amounts.

![Screenshot UG-VI-04 — Against Advance Payment invoice](/images/UG-VI-04.png)

### Against Holdback Amount

Select an earlier PO/CO invoice and release available retained amounts by cost code.

## 9.3 Create an invoice

1. Select **Add Vendor Invoice**.
2. Confirm corporation, project, vendor, and invoice type.
3. Enter invoice number, bill date, due date, and credit terms.
4. Select the PO, CO, advance source, or holdback source when required.
5. Enter type-specific lines.
6. Review advance and holdback adjustments.
7. Review charges, taxes, and Total Invoice Amount.
8. Configure Chart of Account allocation when approval requires it.
9. Add attachments.
10. Save, verify, or approve.

![Screenshot UG-VI-02 — Create / Edit Vendor Invoice form](/images/UG-VI-02.png)

## 9.4 Financial calculation order

For PO/CO invoices:

1. Calculate Invoice Items Total.
2. Deduct only the advance amounts entered in the adjustment fields.
3. Calculate holdback as a percentage of the post-advance item total.
4. Add configured charges and tax.
5. Produce Total Invoice Amount.

An invoice with a **$0 net total is valid** when items are completely offset by advance and/or holdback. An empty item table is a different condition and is not valid.

![Screenshot UG-VI-03 — Invoice items total, charges, tax, and Total Invoice Amount](/images/UG-VI-03.png)

## 9.5 Common validation messages

- Advance adjustment is greater than item total.
- Invoice lines have both zero quantity and zero amount.
- Entered quantity/amount exceeds remaining PO/CO availability.
- Holdback has already been fully invoiced.
- A final invoice must apply the required remaining advance.
- COA allocations are missing or do not match the invoice total.

Correct the highlighted rows or breakdown before saving.

## 9.6 Workflow and statuses

User-facing states include:

- **Pending:** entry-level draft.
- **To be approved:** verified and waiting for approver.
- **Approved:** approved locally and ready for/posting.
- **Rejected:** returned for correction.
- **Unpaid:** posted but not paid.
- **Partially Paid:** some payment received.
- **Paid:** fully paid.
- **Void:** voided in the accounting workflow.

Paid, partially paid, and void invoices are read-only for normal workflow actions.

## 9.7 Payment details

Unpaid invoices become Partially Paid or Paid when payment information is available. The Payment Details popover can show Check Number, Pay Method, Payment Date, Paid Status, and Amount.

Print Checks is currently a placeholder and should not be presented as a completed workflow.

## 9.8 Delete and edit restrictions

- Approved, Paid, Partially Paid, and Void invoices can be locked from deletion.
- An advance invoice cannot be deleted after its distributions are used.
- Existing unpaid invoices may lock advance-adjustment inputs.
- Approval-tier restrictions can hide or block edit/delete.
