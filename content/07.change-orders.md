## Purpose

A Change Order modifies an existing Purchase Order commitment.

## Before you begin

- The original PO must exist.
- Project, vendor, currency, and document context normally come from the PO.

## 7.1 Create a Change Order

**Where to go:** Const Orders → Change Orders

![Screenshot UG-CO-01 — Const Orders Change Orders list](/images/UG-CO-01.png)

1. Select **Add Change Order**.
2. Select the original PO.
3. Confirm project, vendor, and CO type.
4. Confirm the generated CO number.
5. Select a created date on or after the PO entry date.
6. Enter material quantity/price changes or labor amount changes.
7. Review financial impact, charges, and taxes.
8. Add reason, quote reference, instructions, attachments, and revision information.
9. Save or advance through approval.

## 7.2 Material and labor

- Material COs show original PO values beside CO quantity, price, and total.
- Labor COs show original PO amount and additional CO amount.
- Location-wise projects can show changes by location.

## 7.3 Status and restrictions

The Change Orders list displays these status badges:

- **Pending:** being prepared.
- **To be approved:** awaiting approval.
- **Approved:** approved for use.
- **Change order rejected:** returned for correction.
- **Partially Received:** some ordered material has been received.
- **Completed:** all receivable material has been fulfilled.

An active Receipt Note or Vendor Invoice can lock general editing and deletion. Approved or received documents may allow only restricted Ship To or print-option edits.

## 7.4 Print and email

Use Print or Email for customer/vendor communication.
