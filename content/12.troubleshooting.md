## Why is a record missing?

Check corporation, project/vendor filters, status, date range, and pagination. Clear filters and refresh.

## Why is Add/Edit/Delete missing?

The user's role may not permit the action, the document may be approved/verified, or linked records may lock it.

## Why can I not create another estimate?

Only one active estimate is allowed per project. Edit the existing estimate.

## Why can I not import estimate items to a PO?

The estimate may not be Approved, or its quantities may already be consumed by active estimate-import POs.

## Why can I not edit or delete a PO/CO?

Check for linked Receipt Notes, Vendor Invoices, or Change Orders. Also check approval status and approval tier.

## Why can I not save a GRN?

Wait for source lines to load, enter at least one positive received quantity, and correct any quantity above the available leftover.

## Why did the GRN modal close before the success message?

Save & Close intentionally returns to the list immediately. The toast confirms the background save result.

## Why is a vendor invoice total $0?

A $0 net can be correct when advance and/or holdback fully offset invoice items. Confirm there are valid item lines; an empty item table is not the same as a $0 net invoice.

## Why did invoice approval fail?

Review the displayed error. Common causes are a duplicate invoice number, missing or mismatched COA allocation, advance above item total, or a remaining-quantity overrun.

## Why is an approved invoice shown as Unpaid?

Approved describes approval. Unpaid describes payment status. It remains Unpaid until payment is recorded.

## Why is a report empty?

Confirm required Corporation, Project, Vendor, and date filters. Some reports require Approved or paid transactions in the selected period.

## What information should be captured before escalation?

- Corporation and project.
- Module and document number.
- User action attempted.
- Current status.
- Exact validation/toast text.
- Filter values.
- Screenshot with sensitive data removed.
- Time of the issue.
